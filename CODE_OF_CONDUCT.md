# Code of Conduct

Token Heroes is a game, and building it should feel like one: friendly, curious and fair. We
want everyone to be able to join in, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people making their first contribution.
- Critique the work, not the person. Explain the why behind a review comment.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Accept that maintainers sometimes say no, and that they will say why.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling or deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, tell the maintainers privately, through a GitHub
private report on this repository or by contacting a maintainer directly. Every report is read,
kept confidential and answered. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may block
anyone who breaks these rules.

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where
someone represents the project. It is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
