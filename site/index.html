How it works

One burned token is one coin

The game reads the session logs your agents already write on your disk, counts the tokens they burn and credits one coin per token. The fight never pays. Stuck on a boss? Ship more work.

The fight

It plays itself. You decide what to buy.

  • ∞

    Endless stages

    Packs grow by one foe every 10 stages. Every 5th stage is a boss.

  • 10%

    Auto battle

    Heroes attack on their own. Crits hit for 2.5× damage.

  • 50%

    Tap to strike

    Click the arena to hit the front foe for half your party's power.

  • 1M

    Ultimate

    About an hour of agent work charges ten seconds of party damage.

  • ×4

    Milestones

    Every 25 levels a hero's damage and health grow fourfold.

  • 10 s

    Gentle wipes

    A fallen party is back in ten seconds. Levels and coins stay.

  • 8 h

    Offline progress

    Close the window and the party keeps fighting for up to eight hours.

  • 100

    Ascension

    From stage 100, start over with lasting power from your deepest stage.

Ten heroes

Hire a party

Strikers deal damage, tanks hold the party's health and healers restore it. Every hero is stronger per coin than the one before. The rare ones unlock with your lifetime burn: the Paladin takes 800 million tokens.

Thirty bosses

Eight creatures, nine elements

The creature brings the attack: dragons breathe fire, golems slam, wraiths swing a scythe. The element brings the palette and the weather: embers on a lava floor, snow, lightning and rain, a summoning ring.

Privacy

Your tokens stay yours

  • Only the usage numbers are read. Prompts and code are never stored or shown.
  • Your work never leaves your machine. The one request the app makes is the update check on GitHub.
  • Everything sits in ~/.token-heroes, so a reinstall keeps your progress.
  • Only the host adds coins. The window can spend them, never more than you have.
